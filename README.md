# AQ-HEALTH AI – Air Quality & Health Risk Intelligence Platform

**Real-time monitoring, AI-powered disease risk prediction, and health insights for urban environments.**

## Features
- **Live Dashboard**: Real-time sensor updates (WebSocket, 3–5s)  
- **AI Model Integration**: Your `predict_full` for AQI + disease risks  
- **Pixel-Perfect UI**: Dark theme, gauges, donut, carousel, live camera  
- **Dynamic Diseases**: Top-3 random from 8+ per pollutant (incl. temp/humidity)  
- **Alerts & Recommendations**: Ventilation, mask, health risks  
- **Export**: CSV + PDF reports  
- **Responsive**: Desktop + mobile  
- **Deployable**: Docker, Vercel, Railway
